Meet your host

Wine expert Melchior de Seze grew up in Bordeaux surrounded by his own families vineyard. His wine experience is heavily influenced by this family heritage.

Born in France and now based in London, Melchior has worked all over London in various wine establishment and is now based at The Yard Cafe in Palmers Green, hosting evenings and wine tasting events.

Melchior will host a diverse range of wine tasting evenings each month, exploring wines from across the globe. Each tasting delves into a different region or country.
